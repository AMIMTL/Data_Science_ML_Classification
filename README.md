# Data Science in Python: Classification

**Course source:** [Maven Analytics - Data Science in Python: Classification](https://mavenanalytics.io/course/data-science-in-python-classification)

**Instructor:** Chris Bruehl (Analytics Engineer & Lead Python Instructor)  
**Course length:** ~16 hours (18 assignments & solutions, 9 quizzes, 2 skills assessments)  
**Status:** ✅ Completed

## Course Overview
This hands-on, project-based course teaches the foundations for classification modeling in Python. Through a credit risk case study at Maven National Bank, you'll learn to build and evaluate classification models to determine customer credit risk (high, medium, low).

## What You'll Learn

### Data Preparation & EDA
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Feature engineering (scaling, dummy variables, binning)
- Train/test/validation data splits

### Classification Models (scikit-learn)
- **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)** - intuitive distance-based classification
- **Logistic Regression** - coefficient interpretation, probability predictions
- **Decision Trees** - fitting, visualization, and fine-tuning
- **Random Forests** - ensemble method for improved accuracy
- **Gradient Boosted Machines (GBM)** - advanced ensemble technique

### Model Evaluation
- Confusion matrices
- Accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score
- Handling imbalanced data:
  - Threshold tuning
  - Sampling methods (oversampling & SMOTE)
  - Adjusting class weights in cost function

## Course Project: Credit Risk Assessment
You'll play the role of a Data Scientist for the risk management department at Maven National Bank, building models to accurately determine which customers have high, medium, or low credit risk based on their profiles.
